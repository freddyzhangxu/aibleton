# Bossa Nova Production

Use this skill for DAW arranging, instrumentation, MIDI programming, recording, mixing, or style checks. Keep the user's references and intent in control.

## Core sound

Bossa Nova pairs a Samba-derived pulse with Cool Jazz harmony. Its defining quality is restraint: relaxed, intimate, and gently syncopated.

- Use straight eighth notes; keep swing off. Create motion through syncopation and accents away from downbeats.
- Favor major seventh, minor seventh, minor ninth, and sixth chords. Keep voicings warm and avoid harsh tension.
- Center the nylon-string guitar groove. Let bass and light drums support it without competing.

## Arrange

Start with a compact group: nylon-string guitar, acoustic or soft electric bass, and restrained drums. Add piano or intimate vocals only when useful.

- Guitar: gentle syncopated chord accompaniment; avoid forceful strumming and long solos.
- Bass: simple, smooth roots and harmonic extensions; no slap or large leaps.
- Drums: soft, sparse kick; light rimshots; steady, quiet hi-hat; avoid loud crashes and explosive fills.
- Optional piano: gentle seventh-chord fills. Optional vocals: close, breathy, and understated.

## MIDI groove

- Guitar: syncopated eighth-note chords, leaving space on downbeats; MIDI velocity 55–75 with subtle variation.
- Bass: emphasize beats 1 and 3 with a smooth eighth-note line; velocity 60–80.
- Drums: straight eighth-note hi-hat, light rimshot on beats 2 and 4, and soft kick on beats 1 and 3.
- Keep swing off for every part. Adjust these patterns to the user's meter, tempo, and reference.

## Recording and mix

- Nylon guitar: try a small-diaphragm condenser 20–30 cm from the body; capture soft overtones without excessive body resonance. Play gently and control string noise.
- Vocals: record close to the microphone at a low, intimate level. Favor a gentle performance over forceful projection.
- Bass: keep the low end warm but controlled so it does not mask the guitar.
- Mix with gentle compression, clear low frequencies, soft cymbal highs, and modest short reverb. Preserve natural dynamics; avoid loudness maximization and long, expansive reverb.

## Style checklist

- Straight eighths, no swing; relaxed syncopation.
- Restrained performance and dynamics; no explosive drum parts.
- Nylon-string guitar remains the central groove.
- Harmony is warm; bass and drums stay supportive.
- Vocals, if present, are intimate rather than belted.
